Marketing Summary: Inside the LHC is an immersive, first-person 3D walkthrough of the world's largest particle accelerator, built to run instantly in any browser with no install. Viewers ride a proton the way it actually travels — injected at 450 GeV, bent by the 1,232 superconducting dipole magnets at 1.9 K, accelerated to 6.8 TeV, and slammed head-on into another proton inside the ATLAS detector, watched in slow motion. Every number and part is real, explained in plain language as you go, so a student with no physics background can follow it. Three modes — a guided narrated ride, a proton's-eye chase-cam, and free-roam exploration with labeled components — fit classrooms, science-center and museum exhibits, and physics outreach.
